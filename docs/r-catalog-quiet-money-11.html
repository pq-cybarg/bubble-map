Quiet money (batch 12): quiet stablecoin & crypto-infrastructure fortunes

The deliberately opaque fortunes behind stablecoin + crypto plumbing - the quietest money in crypto sits under the whole market.

Tether -> Cantor -> Treasuries -> government

  • Tether (Giancarlo Devasini / Paolo Ardoino, already mapped) is the largest, most opaque stablecoin - and now a top-20-scale holder of US Treasuries via ~$120B+ reserves. Cantor Fitzgerald custodies/manages the bulk of those reserves and took a ~5% (~$600M) stake in Tether.
  • Howard Lutnick built and long ran Cantor; he became US Commerce Secretary in 2025 and handed Cantor to his sons. The chain - opaque stablecoin -> single primary-dealer custodian -> a cabinet secretary's former firm -> Treasury demand - is a documented, politically charged node. Custody, stake, roles, and the divestiture pledge are fact; the conflict-of-interest framing is graded.

Quiet crypto market-making

  • Evgeny Gaevoy -> Wintermute - a top algorithmic crypto market-maker + OTC desk. With Cumberland/Jane Street/Jump, Wintermute is part of the concentrated, low-profile liquidity layer that keeps stablecoins + tokens trading at scale.

Mining ASIC money pivoting to AI

  • Jihan Wu -> Bitmain / Bitdeer - Bitmain's Antminer ASICs are a chokepoint on Bitcoin mining hardware; Wu now leads Bitdeer (Nasdaq-listed), which - like Core Scientific, TeraWulf, IREN - is converting power-rich mining sites into AI datacenters. A quiet, geopolitically sensitive hardware fortune bridging crypto mining to the AI-power buildout.

Honest limits

Ownership, custody, stakes, and roles are fact; exact reserve figures are only partly disclosed; conflict-of-interest and systemic framings are graded. No cabal is imputed - these are rival firms - but the throughline is a method: quiet, concentrated capital sitting under stablecoins, crypto liquidity, and mining/AI compute.

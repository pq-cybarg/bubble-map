Infrastructure-layer deplatforming - the third chokepoint

(Part of the speech-control apparatus layer. The stack has three independent chokepoints on the same target: the money (card networks / processors - Financial_Censorship), the content (state speech law - Online_Speech_Regulation), and the pipe - hosting, app stores, registrars, CDN. This block maps the pipe. Framing is contested throughout; both sides presented.)

The documented cases (fact)

  • AWS / Parler (Jan 2021): Amazon Web Services suspended Parler's hosting after Jan 6; the app was offline ~a month until it found new hosting. The clearest cloud-hosting deplatforming - one IaaS provider can take a lawful service off the internet. AWS cited repeated violent-content ToS violations; critics called it infrastructure censorship without due process.
  • Apple + Google / Parler (Jan 2021): both app stores removed Parler (Apple restored it Apr 2021 after moderation changes). iOS has no sideloading (pre-DMA), so Apple is the sole iOS distribution channel; the two stores are a duopoly gate on mobile apps (structural power documented in Epic v. Apple).
  • Cloudflare (2017-2022): terminated Daily Stormer (Aug 2017), 8chan (Aug 2019), and Kiwi Farms (Sep 2022) - CDN/DDoS withdrawal that can functionally silence a site. Crucial nuance: each time, CEO Matthew Prince published statements calling the intervention dangerous, warning that infrastructure providers policing content sets a bad precedent. The chokepoint is real and its most-used operator says it should not be a speech arbiter.
  • Registrars / DNS: GoDaddy dropped Gab (2018) and AR15.com; registry/registrar seizures (court orders, ICE-adjacent) can remove a domain entirely. Losing the name is losing the address.

Why "infrastructure" is a distinct chokepoint

Each layer is near-essential and concentrated: a handful of IaaS clouds host most of the web; two app stores gate mobile; a few CDNs provide the DDoS protection a large site needs; the DNS/registrar system is a single namespace. Cutting any one can de-platform a target regardless of legality - which is exactly why it is powerful and why it is contested.

Two convergences (the honest analytic points)

  1. Law is deputizing the pipe. The EU DSA reaches hosting/intermediary services; the UK OSA reaches "ancillary" services; app-store age laws push duties onto Apple/Google. State speech law and private infrastructure now reinforce each other. (interpretation, labeled.)
  2. Three parallel chokepoints, not one cabal. A site can be cut at the money (Financial_Censorship), the content (Online_Speech_Regulation), or the pipe (this block). These are distinct, overlapping mechanisms with different operators and motives - the map holds them as parallel hypotheses, and the app-store layer is also where identity/age attestation (Apple Declared Age Range, Play Age Signals) is converging with distribution.

Honest limits

Actions, dates, and the Cloudflare self-statements = fact. "Private anti-abuse / ToS / legal-compliance" vs "infrastructure censorship without due process" = contested (both presented) - and Cloudflare's own stated reluctance is included precisely because it cuts against a simple-censorship read. The stack-convergence + competing-frame points = interpretation, labeled. No coordinated-conspiracy claim is asserted; these are independent operators that can each, alone, silence a target.

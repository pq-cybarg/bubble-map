In-Q-Tel portfolio: public IC-capital edges, not operational control

IQT is a CIA-chartered nonprofit VC, not a directorate and not a joystick. A portfolio edge means: public reporting that IQT put money (or was listed as an investor) in a commercial firm whose tech the IC might later buy. It does not mean IQT runs the product, staffs the SOC, or tasked the APT.

Fact-grade here: CIA charter; Palantir seed; Keyhole -> Google Earth; Recorded Future; Substrate (Reuters).

Strong (named journalism): Dataminr, Lookout, Databricks (Intercept 2016).

Moderate (portfolio databases / secondary): FireEye, Tenable, MongoDB, D-Wave, Rigetti, Infleqtion, Xanadu, Cerebras, Groq, ICEYE, Nozomi, VulnCheck.

Weak: Anduril appears on some third-party IQT holding lists; no IQT press release in this block.

This is still not the full 200-800 company roster. It is the slice that already sits on this landscape (intel, quantum, lithography, AppSec, OT, threat intel) plus the canonical geo/data exhibits.

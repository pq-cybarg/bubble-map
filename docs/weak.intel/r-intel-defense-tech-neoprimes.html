Defense-tech neoprimes: Palantir, Anduril, Shield AI, and In-Q-Tel capital

Four nodes, three planes:

  • Palantir - data/C2 software (Gotham, Foundry, Maven). Public. Early In-Q-Tel seed is history.
  • Anduril - Lattice plus the hardware Lattice talks to. TITAN and Golden Dome C2 appear in 2026 press; Golden Dome consortium naming is unnamed-source (graded moderate).
  • Shield AI - Hivemind (the autopilot), V-BAT, Tracker. Company-stated work on Anduril's Fury CCA and a 2024 Palantir UAV partnership.
  • In-Q-Tel - IC-chartered VC. Palantir seed + Substrate lithography. A relevance signal, not a joystick.

Do not collapse these into one "Thiel machine." Founders Fund is an edge, not a mind. Dual-use for Shield AI here means the *product* (autonomy/UAV) has military and border-surveillance buyers in public reporting.

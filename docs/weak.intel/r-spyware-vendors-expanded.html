Mercenary spyware, hack-for-hire, and the forensics that expose them

Entities (7)

NameTypeNotes
Gamma_GroupvendorGamma International (UK/Germany); FinFisher GmbH, Munich. Built the FinFisher/FinSpy 'lawful intercept' suite - full-device implants. FinFisher GmbH filed for insolvency 2022 after German export-law prosecution.
FinSpymalwareGamma's modular implant (calls, messages, mic/camera, files). Amnesty (Sep 2020) found new variants targeting Egyptian civil society and first publicly revealed FinSpy for macOS and Linux alongside Windows/Android.
Hacking_TeamvendorMilan; 'Remote Control System' / Galileo, sold to abusive governments. Itself hacked in 2015 (400GB dump) confirming client lists incl. Sudan/Ethiopia. Wound down / reformed as Memento Labs.
CandiruvendorTel Aviv (aka Sourgum). 'DevilsTongue' Windows spyware used against civil society across multiple countries. Added to US Commerce Entity List (2021) alongside NSO.
Hack_For_HirecapabilityThe mercenary phishing/hack-for-hire market. 'Dark Basin' (Citizen Lab, 2020) tied an operation targeting thousands (climate advocates, journalists) to India-based BellTroX - distinct from implant vendors but same clientele.
ApplevendorSued NSO (2021) after FORCEDENTRY and shipped Lockdown Mode; later moved to dismiss (2024) to protect threat-intel methods. Platform defender with dual incentives.
MetavendorMeta/WhatsApp's 2019 suit over a zero-click (1,400 targets) produced a 2025 US jury verdict against NSO (~$167M punitive, later adjusted) - the first major courtroom loss for a spyware vendor.

Quiet money (batch 13): the neocloud picks-and-shovels founders

The map's financial core maps the AI capital loop at the firm level (NVIDIA / hyperscalers / neoclouds). This batch adds the lower-profile founder fortunes behind the compute "picks and shovels" - the people who got rich building the rented GPU layer, rather than the famous lab founders.

CoreWeave - the commodities desk that became a neocloud

  • Michael Intrator -> CoreWeave - co-founder/CEO of CoreWeave (NASDAQ: CRWV). The three founders ran Hudson Ridge, a natural-gas/commodities hedge fund, then built CoreWeave out of an Ethereum GPU-mining operation before pivoting to GPU cloud - a quiet commodities-desk origin now central to AI compute; large paper fortune after the Mar-2025 IPO.
  • Brian Venturo -> CoreWeave - co-founder + long-time technical chief (CTO/CSO), ex-Hudson Ridge.
  • Brannin McBee -> CoreWeave - co-founder (Chief Strategy/Development Officer), the third ex-commodities founder.

Lambda - the long-running developer GPU cloud

  • Stephen Balaban -> Lambda - co-founded Lambda (Lambda Labs) in 2012 and still runs it; GPU cloud + deep-learning workstations/servers that rode AI-compute demand into large private rounds. A low-profile founder-CEO fortune.

Crusoe - stranded energy to AI compute

  • Chase Lochmiller -> Crusoe Energy - co-founder/CEO; turned flared natural gas into on-site compute (bitcoin first, then AI) and pivoted into large AI data centers (incl. the Abilene "Stargate" campus). An energy-to-compute founder fortune at the AI power bottleneck.
  • Cully Cavness -> Crusoe Energy - co-founder (President/COO), the energy-side partner in the flare-gas-to-datacenter story.

The convergence point: identity

The layers are drifting toward a single meeting point - identity. Age/ID attestation (law), device + app-store attestation (pipe), ID+payment rails (upstream DPI), and money+identity chokepoints (finance) all push the same object: identify the person first, then decide who may speak, associate, or transact. That is why the Digital-ID thread sits at the center of this map.

The doctrine (why this is NOT a conspiracy claim)

  1. Different operators, different motives. A card network cutting a merchant, a regulator fining a platform, a cloud dropping a host, and a foreign troll farm are not the same actor and mostly do not coordinate. Treating them as one hand is the error this project refuses (see the composition-fallacy and apophenia guards).
  2. Parallel, overlapping explanations. Some de-platforming/pressure activity the EA/AI-network map does not explain is better explained by one of these other layers - finance, law, infrastructure, or influence ops. They are competing hypotheses, and often several are true at once for the same event.
  3. Attribution discipline scales with distance from the act. The downstream acts (a specific takedown) are facts; the upstream agenda (WEF/DPI) is the weakest to attribute causally and is deliberately under-claimed; covert "potential" ops are speculation, always labeled.
  4. Reversibility matters. OnlyFans and itch.io reversed under counter-pressure; a statute does not. The apparatus is not monolithic - it has give in some layers and none in others, and that texture is part of the honest picture.

Honest limits

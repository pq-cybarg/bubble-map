Quiet money (batch 9): the secretive prop-trading & market-making fortunes

The quietest large fortunes in finance are the proprietary-trading + market-making firms - private partnerships that take no outside capital, publish nothing, and yet intermediate a huge share of both TradFi order flow and crypto liquidity.

The firms + their (reclusive) principals

  • Rob Granieri -> Jane Street - the last active co-founder of a deliberately opaque partnership that became one of the most profitable trading firms on earth (dominant in ETFs, options, and crypto). The Jane Street -> Alameda/FTX talent pipeline (Sam Bankman-Fried, Caroline Ellison) seeded the biggest crypto blow-up - lineage only, no imputation of Jane Street culpability.
  • Bill DiSomma -> Jump Trading - co-founder (with Paul Gurinas) of a secretive Chicago HFT/prop giant. Its Jump Crypto arm is a top crypto market-maker + infra builder (it wrote the Firedancer Solana validator client; central to the Terra/Wormhole episodes).
  • Don Wilson -> DRW - founder of a low-profile Chicago prop firm (futures, fixed income, real estate, crypto). Its Cumberland arm is one of the largest OTC digital-asset market-makers (a reported 2024 SEC Wells notice is contested).

Why they matter

Jump, Citadel Securities, Jane Street, DRW, Susquehanna, Hudson River and Virtu are the mostly-private market-makers that quietly run market plumbing - and the same firms (via Jump Crypto, Cumberland, Jane Street, Wintermute) provide the bulk of institutional crypto liquidity. A concentrated, near-invisible layer sits under both markets.

Honest limits

Founder roles and firm scope are documented facts; exact profits, market shares, and the Cumberland Wells-notice status are private/contested/graded. The lineage into Alameda/FTX is factual but carries no imputation of intent. The throughline is a method - quiet, concentrated, private trading capital - not a coordinated plan.

Story -> DATA Foundation + the Aria / ARIAIP / APL IP-RWA stack (Kostiner)

An a16z-backed L1 that has quietly changed what it is.

Story -> The DATA Foundation (the pivot)

Story Protocol launched (Feb 2025) as "put intellectual property on-chain" - a Programmable IP License plus a "Proof of Creativity," with David Kostiner as co-founding partner & Chief IP Officer, built by PIP Labs (Pen Technology Inc.), led by a ~$80M+ a16z Crypto raise.

It did not find product-market fit. On/around June 25, 2026 the project rebranded to The DATA Foundation and pivoted from IP to AI training data - explicitly because "labs have effectively run out of internet to scrape." The $IP token migrates to $DATA 1:1 (1B supply carried forward); Story Network becomes DATA Network; new pieces are Trace (on-chain data-provenance receipts), Poseidon (model-ready datasets), and contributor apps Numo / Kled (Kled added ~1.5B user-consented records). Andrea Muttoni becomes CEO; founder SY Lee stays as adviser; Kled's Avi Patel joins as chief data officer.

That pivot puts this cluster inside the map's own AI-data / provenance thesis: when permissionless IP licensing collided with the control rightsholders actually want, the value migrated to selling opt-in human data to AI labs.

The health picture (your "quiet for months" read - confirmed)

  • $IP peaked ~$14.78 (Sept 2025); now ~98% lower (~$500M FDV).
  • Less than $100/day in on-chain revenue.
  • The Feb 13 2026 unlock was delayed to Aug 13 2026 (SIP-00009/00010) amid supply-overhang fears and "usage remains thin."
  • Aria/IPRWA payouts are early-stage with limited public reporting.

Per the map's doctrine, thin activity + quiet is a risk signal, not proof of wrongdoing - it cuts both ways.

Aria Protocol (built on Story) - the IP-RWA product

Aria fractionalizes real IP into IPRWA tokens. $ARIAIP (1B supply) is the coordination token (Binance Alpha listing/airdrop Nov 7 2025, on Story + BSC). $APL was the first product (2025) - partial royalty rights across ~48 songs (Justin Bieber, Miley Cyrus, BLACKPINK, BTS). In 2026 Aria added its first newly issued master, the RESCENE x The MUZE Entertainment single "Busy Boy," routing royalties to $APL holders. IP Strategy (Nasdaq: IPST) publicly promotes these releases. Aria raised ~$15M (Polychain, Neoclassic, Story Foundation).

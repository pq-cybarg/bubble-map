CikLeak claims breach of Russia's Central Election Commission + GAS Vybory 2.0 (Sept 2026)

On 16 Sep 2026, a group calling itself CikLeak claimed it breached Russia's Central Election Commission (CEC) and its contractors - reportedly obtaining classified documents, server configurations, passwords, source code, meeting recordings, and employee chats. Among the contractors it names is the developer of GAS Vybory 2.0, Russia's new election system slated for first use in the upcoming State Duma elections.

The hackers also published an interactive "game" using what they say are the system's real interfaces, in which the player is an election official who changes the results, and called on commission members to report fraud to them. They say they did not interfere with voting. Ironically, CEC head Ella Pamfilova had said in August 2026 that the system was "impossible to hack."

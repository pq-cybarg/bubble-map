Quiet money (batch 4): Gulf AI/surveillance capital, crypto's quiet controllers, political kingmakers

The remaining thin areas of the quiet-money map: foreign state-capital and crypto's opaque controllers.

Gulf state-capital-surveillance fusion

The most concentrated fusion of intelligence + AI/data + capital anywhere - hedging the US against China.

Crypto's quiet controllers

  • Giancarlo Devasini (chairman/largest owner) + Paolo Ardoino (CEO) run Tether - the ~$140B+ stablecoin issuer whose profits rival major banks, which holds $100B+ in US Treasuries and plows profit into Bitcoin/gold/compute - a private, offshore, systemically-entangled dollar rail with famously opaque governance.

Quiet political kingmakers

  • Harold Hamm (Continental Resources) - shale pioneer + "drill baby drill" energy-policy kingmaker.

Quantum-ready reference: QRL

—

The Quantum Resistant Ledger is the only large-theme L1 in this corpus that was post-quantum at genesis (mandatory XMSS, NIST SP 800-208). Bitcoin, Ethereum, and almost every other production chain still authorize spend with ECDSA/Schnorr — harvest-now, forge-later. QRI Stage 4 is a cryptographic-readiness fact. It is not an adoption, liquidity, or token-accrual claim (altcoin-lens remains the utility axis; spec-blockchain-ecosystem is the TNFL write-up).

How to read the three axes

Public-record flags (cards) = court, OFAC, insolvency, custody, listing. QRI (pills / table) = third-party quantum-attack readiness of production cryptography. altcoin-lens = institutional utility vs token-accrual. A Stage-4 chain can be illiquid. A NYDFS-licensed CEX can be fully ECC-exposed. FTX’s conviction is not a property of Ethereum.

ECDSA/Schnorr over secp256k1 is the default spend-auth of BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, and most L2s. A cryptographically relevant quantum computer derives the private key from a revealed public key. Address reuse and P2PK outputs make that exposure permanent. That is the harvest-now / forge-later (TNFL) problem in the ecosystem block — QRL does not have it on native spend.

# Notable cyber individuals (batch 2): leakers, malware authors, the informant - + Mirai

Second batch of named, public-record cyber figures wired to their events/orgs, with graded dossiers in the Persons tab. Also adds the **Mirai** IoT-botnet node (a major malware that was missing).

- **Chelsea Manning** -> WikiLeaks - Army analyst who leaked the War Logs / Collateral Murder / Cablegate (2010); convicted 2013, commuted 2017.
- **Josh Schulte** -> Vault 7 - ex-CIA engineer convicted (2022) as the source of the largest data theft in CIA history (also a separate CSAM conviction, stated as court fact).
- **Onel de Guzman** -> ILOVEYOU - the Manila student who wrote the 2000 worm; arrested but never charged (no applicable law); confirmed authorship on record in 2020.
- **Paras Jha** -> **Mirai** - co-author of the IoT botnet (pleaded guilty 2017); built for Minecraft DDoS markets before it escaped.
- **Hector Monsegur ("Sabu")** -> LulzSec - a leader who, after his 2011 arrest, became an **FBI informant** whose cooperation dismantled the crew.

## Mirai (2016)
Enslaved insecure IoT devices (default credentials) into a massive DDoS botnet - hit Krebs-on-Security, OVH, and via **Dyn** (Oct 2016) knocked Twitter/Netflix/Reddit offline across the US East Coast. Its **source code was released publicly**, spawning endless variants - the IoT-botnet branch of the malware lineage and a wake-up call on IoT security + DNS single-points-of-failure.

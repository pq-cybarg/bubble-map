# Stablecoin / GENIUS Act update (late 2026) - the regulatory moat + the Treasury bid

*(Update to the stablecoin threads. A year after the GENIUS Act, the market is bigger and more concentrated, and regulation is sorting issuers into "federally-supervised US" vs "offshore, carved out of US/EU retail.")*

## The law + the market (fact)
- **GENIUS Act** (signed **18 Jul 2025**): 100% cash/T-bill reserves, monthly disclosures, audits, **no interest** on balances; issuers **>$10B** fall under **OCC** federal supervision.
- Market **~$322.6B** (May 2026): **USDT ~$189.5B**, **USDC ~$78.8B**; **Tether + Circle ~83%**.

## Implementation: slow, then a Treasury move (fact)
- **Seven** federal agencies missed the **18 Jul 2026** rulemaking deadline. OCC proposed its rule **25 Feb 2026**, final committed **by Nov 2026**.
- **Treasury's interim final rule (30 Sep 2026)** is the first **binding** GENIUS rule: it certifies **state** stablecoin regimes, but the **$10B threshold locks Tether and Circle out of the state pathway** (into federal OCC supervision). The **ABA** warns state-certified frameworks could open an arbitrage.

## Winners and the split (fact; "moat" = interpretation)
- **Circle** built a **regulatory moat**: OCC-approved **Circle National Trust** (federal charter), MiCA compliance, stock **+35%** (late Feb 2026). USDC reframed from experiment to regulated infrastructure.
- **Tether** stays offshore with **USDT**, but launched **USAT** (Jan 2026, issued by **Anchorage Digital Bank**) for US compliance.
- **MiCA** delisted **USDT** from EU venues (Binance/Coinbase/Kraken EEA retail) while authorizing **USDC/EURC** (**+337%** EU volume). Hong Kong issued its first licenses (**HSBC, Anchorpoint**, Apr 2026).
- **Rails:** Cross River + Lead Bank settle **USDC over Solana with Visa**.

## The macro tell (fact)
Stablecoin issuers now hold **~$155B in US Treasury bills - more than Germany + Saudi Arabia + South Korea combined.** The reserve mandate makes the sector a **structural, price-insensitive Treasury buyer** - the clearest line from "crypto" to the Treasury market the macro threads track.

## Honest limits
Law, rules, dates, and market figures = **fact**. "Regulatory moat" / "US-vs-offshore consolidation" = **interpretation**, labeled. The Treasury-demand channel is real (the holdings are fact); its market impact is a magnitude the block does not overclaim.

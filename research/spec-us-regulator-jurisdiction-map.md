# US financial/tech/defense regulator jurisdiction map — the oversight backbone (and the fragmentation that enables arbitrage)

The recurring agencies across this corpus were each wired only via one-off enforcement edges. This overlay adds the **structural "who regulates what"** backbone — and makes a point beyond completeness: the **fragmentation itself** (overlapping, ambiguous jurisdiction) is the arbitrage surface actors exploit.

## The map (statutory jurisdiction = fact; capture/arbitrage = labeled interpretation)

- **Banking (deliberately fragmented):** Federal Reserve (holding-company supervision, monetary policy), **OCC** (national-bank charter), FDIC (deposit insurance), **CFPB** (consumer protection), **NCUA** (credit unions). Five overlapping chartering/insurance regimes = a regulatory-arbitrage surface.
- **Accounting standards — the quiet lever:** **FASB** sets GAAP, including the **HTM-at-amortized-cost** rule (banks carry underwater bonds at cost — the self-marked bank-asset defect) and **useful-life/depreciation** (the knob behind the AI-capex depreciation trap).
- **Housing:** **FHFA** is conservator of Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac (Director Pulte drove the Fannie x Palantir tie-up).
- **Retirement:** **DOL** sets ERISA fiduciary rules — the gate a 2025 EO pried open to push private credit/alts into 401(k)s (the retailization risk).
- **Crypto/markets (the SEC-vs-CFTC ambiguity):** SEC (securities), CFTC (commodities), FinCEN (BSA/AML), OFAC (sanctions), Treasury (GENIUS-Act stablecoins + FSOC/OFR systemic watch). The Binance $4.3B (2023) resolution spanned DOJ/FinCEN/OFAC/CFTC at once.
- **Tech/competition:** FTC (antitrust + connected-car privacy).
- **Compute export control:** **Commerce/BIS** (chip export licenses, entity list, FDP rule) — the US half of the two-sided chip-chokepoint war.
- **Homeland/defense:** **DHS** (parent of ICE → Palantir ImmigrationOS/data brokers); **Department of War** (renamed from DoD, 2025; ~$1T/yr; buyer of the primes, Palantir Maven, Anduril — and, via the IDScan breach, whose Secretary's ID was among 153M exposed).

## Not exhaustive
This wires the recurring financial/tech/defense regulators. Others touch the corpus and can be added as their blocks are interconnected — FCC (telecom), EPA, FAA (Boeing), HHS/CDC, GAO/OMB (oversight/budget), PCAOB (audit), State/GEC.

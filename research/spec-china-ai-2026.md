# China AI update (2026) - DeepSeek V4 on Huawei silicon, compute substitution, training closure

*(Update to the China-AI threads. The 2026 story is the pivot to domestic chips - and a parallel move to close the open-weight door from the state side.)*

## DeepSeek V4 -> Huawei (fact; R2 is the unresolved test)
**DeepSeek V4** (2026) was optimized to run **exclusively on Huawei Ascend** (950/910D) - a sharp break from its Nvidia-H800-trained earlier models and the **first frontier-scale endorsement** of Ascend. **ByteDance, Alibaba, Tencent** then ordered **hundreds of thousands** of Ascend chips. The open question is **R2**: if trained fully on domestic silicon, it proves frontier models can be built without Nvidia; if it still needs Nvidia, the dependency narrative survives.

## Huawei's rise, Nvidia's collapse (fact)
- **Huawei ~50-60%** of China's AI-chip market (from ~nothing three years ago); ~**750k Ascend 950PR** planned for 2026; the **910D** is now the data-center backbone.
- **Nvidia's China share fell from ~95% (2023) to ~8% (2026)** under export controls - "a policy meant to slow China's buildout minted its chip champion" (analysts' framing).
- **Limits:** stuck at **7nm**, dependent on **stockpiled foreign HBM**. Biren + Moore Threads advancing; Alibaba T-Head + Baidu Kunlun ship CUDA-compatible parts.

## The policy vise, both directions (fact)
- **US:** BIS moved **H200** exports to case-by-case (Jan 2026, 9 certifications, cap + no-weight-transfer); treats **Huawei-Ascend use anywhere** as a **presumptive EAR violation** (May 2026). USTR said **no H200 deliveries** had occurred despite clearances - a live but fluid constraint.
- **China:** Beijing began deliberations (Jul 2026) to **restrict overseas access to leading Chinese models including open-weight Qwen**, with labs drifting to **closed-source** - "training closure." It certified **9 domestic AI processors** for government procurement.

## The capability gap (named evaluators, not settled truth)
**NIST CAISI**: DeepSeek **V4 Pro ~8 months** behind the frontier (May 2026); **GLM-5.2 ~comparable** to a US model ~6 months older (Opus 4.6, Jul 2026). **Closing, not closed** - and these are the evaluator's assessments, graded as such.

## The synthesis
Beijing's 2026 program reads as **two converging moves**: **total compute substitution** (domestic silicon end-to-end) and **training closure** (closed ecosystems + export limits on its own models). Indigenization and control as one strategy - the same state-alignment logic the earlier China-AI-censorship block mapped, now extended to hardware and to the open-weight door itself.

## Honest limits
Releases, market shares, policy actions, and the 9 certifications = **fact**. R2's fully-domestic training = **unresolved**. The capability-gap numbers = the **named evaluators' assessments** (NIST CAISI), not settled truth. The "one program" synthesis = **interpretation**, labeled; absolute US-China gaps are contested.

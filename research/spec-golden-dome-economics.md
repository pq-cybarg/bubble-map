# Golden Dome economics - the $1.2T estimate, the 6x gap, and the SpaceX conflict

*(Defense / macro thread. Deepens the Golden_Dome node with the numbers - and shows how the program's biggest line item points straight at a Project Meridian co-director's company.)*

## The number, and how it moved (fact)
The nonpartisan **CBO (May 2026)** estimated a Golden-Dome-like shield at **~$1.2 trillion** to develop, deploy, and operate over **20 years**:
- **>2x** CBO's own **2025** figure (**$542B**), and **~7x** the **White House's** May-2025 number (**$175B**).
- Breakdown: **~$740B** space-based warning/tracking + interceptors; **~$270B** ground layers; **~$180B** sensors/R&D/other.
- The **space-based interceptor layer alone is ~70% of acquisition / ~60% of total cost.**
- CBO flagged its figure as **"illustrative"** - there is **no public plan** from the White House or Pentagon detailed enough to cost precisely.

## The 6x gap (fact of the gap; which is right = unknowable without a plan)
Space Force **Gen. Michael Guetlein** (program lead) estimated **~$185B** (Mar 2026) - roughly **one-sixth** of CBO's number. CBO's read: either the Pentagon's architecture is **far more limited** than Trump's executive order describes, or it expects **"significant funding from other accounts."** The honest state is **a trillion-dollar range** with no public baseline.

## The money, already flowing (fact)
- **$24.4B** in the **One Big Beautiful Bill Act** (Jul 2025) + **$22.5B** reconciliation (Mar 2026) - **~90% obligated in under six months.**
- The **FY2027** defense request is a record **~$1.5T**, with **~$750B** earmarked for **missile defense + drones + AI + industrial base**. Golden Dome anchors the topline.
- Timeline: EO in the first week in office; **"operational before end of term"** (Jan 2029); space interceptors targeted **2028**.

## What it can and can't do (fact)
CBO's modeled four-tier system (space + two surface-interceptor layers + spread interceptors) covers **CONUS + Alaska + Hawaii** and could engage **multiple simultaneous** hypersonic/ballistic/cruise missiles - **but not a large-scale attack from a peer** (Russia or China). The shield is sized for rogue-state / limited strikes, not great-power exchange.

## The conflict, with a dollar figure (interpretation, labeled)
The **space layer (~$740B, ~70% of cost)** favors **new-space** - and **SpaceX/Starlink** already flies **~7,000 satellites**, a scale comparable to a limited space-based missile-defense constellation. Meanwhile **Elon Musk co-directs the Pentagon's Project Meridian** capabilities study. A panel co-director advising on priorities **adjacent to his own firm's largest potential contract** is the sharpest instance of the revolving-door concern from the Meridian and defense-tech blocks. Sen. **Jeff Merkley** called the program **"a massive giveaway to defense contractors paid for entirely by working Americans"** (cited critique, labeled). Legacy primes (**Lockheed, RTX, L3Harris**) still contest the **~$270B** ground/sensor layers - the spend splits between the old and new industrial bases.

## Honest limits
CBO/White House/Pentagon numbers, the funding vehicles, the timeline, and the capability limits = **fact**. The 6x gap is **real but unresolved** (no public plan). "Giveaway" = **cited critique**, labeled. The SpaceX-positioning and the Musk/Meridian conflict = **interpretation**, labeled - a documented structural conflict, not an allegation of wrongdoing. The bond-supply tie is a labeled fraction, not the deficit's driver.

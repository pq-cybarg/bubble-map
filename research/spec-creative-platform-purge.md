# The creative-platform purge wave - activist to payment-processor pressure on art & game storefronts

*(Part of the speech-control apparatus layer. A live, concrete instance of the payment-processor chokepoint (see Financial_Censorship) turned on **creative expression** - and a case study in how the money-rail, activist campaigns, and the age-verification climate combine.)*

## The trigger and the letter (fact)
In **July 2025**, the Australian anti-porn group **Collective Shout** published an **open letter to PayPal, Visa, and Mastercard**, demanding they stop processing for platforms hosting NSFW games - citing content involving "rape, incest and child sexual abuse" and a trigger title, **"No Mercy."** It is the same **activist -> payment-processor** pressure channel used by **NCOSE** (the two are peers in tactic).

## The takedowns (fact - the platforms named the cause)
- **Steam / Valve** (~132m monthly users) removed **hundreds** of adult titles about a week later, stating some games "may violate the **rules and standards set forth by our payment processors** and their related card networks and banks," and that "loss of payment methods would prevent customers from being able to purchase other titles." The storefront itself pointed at the money rail.
- **itch.io** **de-indexed all adult NSFW content** from browse/search on **31 July 2025**, saying it "came under scrutiny from our **payment processors**" and had to "prioritize our relationship with our payment partners." It **later began re-indexing** free adult content and slowly reintroducing paid content after mass user call-in campaigns - an **honest counter-example**: like the OnlyFans reversal, some of it proved reversible under counter-pressure.

## What the processors said (contested motive)
The financial companies were careful: **Stripe** - "we do not support adult content"; **Mastercard** - it "has not evaluated any game" and allows all "lawful purchases" but requires merchant controls against illegal content; **PayPal** - it acts on anything violating "the policies of our partner banks and card networks." **None admitted Collective Shout's pressure swayed them.** So the *takedowns* are fact and the platforms *blamed* processors (fact of their statements), but the *coordination/motive* is **contested**.

## Clip Studio Assets / CELSYS (Sep 2026) - same wave, different stated reason
On **23 September 2026**, **Clip Studio Assets** (CELSYS, the marketplace for Clip Studio Paint) **apologized** for a wave of creator materials being "**set to private**," promised to review and republish, and announced it is building the platform to include **"age restrictions, regional controls, and user preferences"** so creators can publish "to the appropriate audiences." CELSYS framed this as **moderation for a mixed-age, multi-region global audience** - it did **not** cite payment processors. So:
- **Fact:** an art-tool marketplace is now building **age + geo-gating** into content distribution.
- **Interpretation (labeled):** it fits the *same 2025-26 wave* of creative-platform moderation under a heightened age-verification / speech-regulation climate. The specific join to **payment-processor** pressure is *not* asserted for CSP - CELSYS's stated driver is audience moderation.

## Why this matters to the map
This is the **clearest current-events demonstration** of the competing-explanation point: de-platforming and content purges have **multiple independent drivers** - the **financial chokepoint** (Steam/itch, processor-named), **activist campaigns** (Collective Shout / NCOSE), and the **age-verification / speech-regulation climate** (UK OSA, AU eSafety, EU) - some of it reversible, some of it self-imposed pre-emption (CSP). It is **not reducible to the EA/AI-safety networks**, and it is **not one cabal**: it is parallel, overlapping pressures converging on the same outcome (creators lose reach / income for legal expression).

## Honest limits
Takedowns, dates, and direct quotes = **fact**. The **CSP tie** to payment pressure is **not asserted** (CELSYS cited age/regional moderation) - only the tie to the broader wave is drawn, and labeled interpretation.

# Grokipedia - the single-owner AI encyclopedia vs the open human one

*As of 2026-10-19. Companion to `spec-grokipedia-info-epistemics.json`. Overlay/structural edges only; the AI-financial SCC is unchanged. Grading is per-claim: fact | reported | interpretation. (Wikipedia and the Wikimedia Foundation are added as graph nodes here - Wikipedia was cited across the corpus as a source but was never itself a node.)*

## What launched

On **October 27, 2025**, xAI launched **Grokipedia** (v0.1) - an encyclopedia whose articles are written and curated by the **Grok** language model rather than by human volunteers. Musk announced it in late September after repeatedly attacking Wikipedia as ideologically biased ("woke"), and positioned Grokipedia as a "maximally truth-seeking" neutral alternative. The debut crashed within hours; the site logged about **885,279 articles** at launch (against **7M+** on English Wikipedia).

## The dependency and the irony (fact)

Despite being pitched as a replacement, many Grokipedia entries were **adapted or copied directly from Wikipedia** - some word-for-word - and some carried a notice: "content adapted from Wikipedia, licensed under CC-BY-SA 4.0." Musk said he wants Grok to stop using Wikipedia as a source by the end of 2025. Wikimedia's response captured it: *"Wikipedia's knowledge is - and always will be - human... even Grokipedia needs Wikipedia to exist."* The AI "alternative" is bootstrapped on the human corpus it aims to replace.

## The editorial model (fact)

The two differ fundamentally:

- **Wikipedia** - open, volunteer-edited, consensus-driven, anyone can edit.
- **Grokipedia** - **no human authors**; articles "fact-checked" by Grok; the public cannot edit, only *suggest* via a form. A February 2026 Tow Center / Columbia Journalism Review analysis called the process **"centrally controlled,"** reporting that AI-generated edits overtook human submissions (**>75%** of proposed changes) by December 2025.

## The bias question (reported / open)

Musk framed it as the fix for Wikipedia bias. Early reporting found the reverse: a **Musk-friendly / right-leaning slant** and **omission** of material unflattering to Musk - reporting cited the absence of his inauguration-gesture controversy and of the **xAI Memphis** gas-turbine/air-quality issue (`spec-xai-colossus-power`). These are **reported analyses**, attributed, not a verdict. The deeper open question an academic comparison raised: whether an AI-driven alternative can escape the biases of the human-edited platform it draws from at all.

## Why it's in the map (interpretation, labeled)

The reference-knowledge layer is infrastructure for what people take to be true. A **single owner** running a **centrally-generated, non-open** encyclopedia - marketed as neutral but exhibiting owner-aligned slant and omission - is an **information/epistemic-control** vector: the commercial, Western mirror image of the state-aligned reference control mapped in `spec-china-ai-stack-censorship`. No intent to deceive is asserted; the concern is the **concentration plus editorial opacity**, not a finding.

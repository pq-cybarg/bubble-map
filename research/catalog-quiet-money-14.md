# Quiet money (batch 14): the commodity-trading houses

The map tracks oil, OPEC+, and sanctions at the state level. This batch adds the **privately-held physical traders** who move the actual barrels - among the **largest and least-visible fortunes in global energy**, and the plumbing beneath the headline price.

## The houses and their quiet owners
- **Torbjorn Tornqvist -> Gunvor** - co-founded 2000, controls one of the largest independent energy traders. A multi-billion fortune on a private firm.
- **Marco Dunand + Daniel Jaeggi -> Mercuria** - co-founded 2004; two low-profile billionaire founders of a top-5 independent trader (crude, products, gas, metals, power).
- **Trafigura** - founded 1993, **employee-owned** across 1,000+ partners (exec chair Jeremy Weir); the fortune is **distributed**, not one owner - central to global oil + metals flows.
- **Vitol** - the world's largest independent oil trader, **partner-owned** and deliberately private: arguably the quietest mega-fortune in energy.

## The Gunvor-Russia history (fact + contested)
Gunvor was co-founded with **Gennady Timchenko**, a Putin associate. Timchenko **sold his ~44% stake to Tornqvist the day before US sanctions (20 Mar 2014)**. The US Treasury's 2014 statement alleged Putin "may have investments in / access to" Gunvor funds; **the firm strongly denied it** and has since cut Russia exposure. Both the timed sale (fact) and the allegation-vs-denial (contested) are presented - not glossed either way.

## Why they're on the map
Independent traders sit **between OPEC+ production and end-markets**: their physical flows, storage, and freight shape realized prices and drive **sanctions-redirection** (e.g. Russian crude under the price cap finding new buyers). Enormous economic leverage, almost no public profile - the definition of quiet money.

## Honest position
Founding and ownership structure = **fact**. Net-worth figures = **estimates** (private firms), graded as such. The Gunvor/Timchenko episode is presented with both the documented facts and the denied allegation. No cabal imputed - these are competing firms - but the throughline is the method: the biggest energy fortunes are the ones you never read about.

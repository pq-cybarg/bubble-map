# CoinEx orderly wind-down (Sept 2026) - the honest counter-example to the exit-scam pattern

On **14-15 Sep 2026**, Haipo Yang (founder of **CoinEx** and the **ViaBTC** mining pool) announced that CoinEx will **cease operations in an orderly wind-down**. This is notable precisely because it is the **honorable opposite** of the pop-up-shell exit-scam pattern the map documents elsewhere ([[spec-binance-exit-scam]] / `Crypto_Exit_Scam`).

## The announced terms (fact)
- **Reserve ratio >100%** (self-claimed); **withdrawals open** from announcement **until 22 Dec 2026** - the 9-year anniversary of CoinEx's 22 Dec 2017 launch.
- **CET buyback** at the **0.005 USDT initial listing price, uncapped**; remaining CET **auto-repurchased from 29 Sep 2026** - returning token holders to entry price rather than zeroing them.
- **Graduated schedule:** 15 Sep registrations halt / futures reduce-only; 22 Sep non-spot services + deposits end; 29 Sep spot trading stops + CET auto-repurchase; **22 Dec 2026** withdrawals end + platform closes.
- Stated reasons: prolonged downturn, falling volume, and **"continuously rising regulatory requirements...beyond reasonable boundaries"** - "carrying **unlimited risk for limited revenue** is no longer rational." Yang says he **declined to sell** the platform, preferring a clean end over handing user trust to a new owner.

## Honest grading
- The **announced terms + schedule are fact** (stated by the founder + the official announcement).
- The **>100% reserve is a self-claim** - contested-until-withdrawals-actually-clear. The "honest exit" reading is **interpretation**, contingent on execution.
- A **real sting remains**: unwithdrawn **USDT after 22 Dec 2026 enters custody at a 5%/month fee** (final claims deadline 22 Aug 2028) - so stragglers are penalized, and the clock matters.

## Why it belongs on the map
Two threads meet here: (1) a **counter-example** to `Crypto_Exit_Scam` - if it executes as stated, a full-reserve, withdrawals-honored, token-bought-back closure is how a responsible exchange dies; and (2) a **compliance-cost-squeeze** data point - a nine-year-old mid-tier CEX concluding that regulatory + security risk now exceeds the revenue that justifies it.

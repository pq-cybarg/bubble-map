# The venture-defense capital wave - new primes, "American Dynamism", and the revolving door

*(Defense / capital thread. The Project Meridian block put the cluster's founders inside the Pentagon; this block maps the cluster itself - who built it, who funds it, and the conflict baked into both.)*

## The exemplar: Anduril (fact; the $100B is reported)
- **$5B Series H at a $61B valuation** (May 2026, led by **Thrive Capital + a16z**) - double the **$30.5B** round a year earlier (led by **Founders Fund**).
- In talks toward **~$100B** (two-stage; secondary market already implied ~$100B by Sep 2026). Revenue **~doubled to ~$2.25B** (2025). Opened **Arsenal-1**, a **$900M / 5M-sq-ft** Ohio plant (Mar 2026). **>$11B** raised total; Founders Fund's **$1B** Series G check was reportedly its largest ever.
- Founded 2017 by **Palantir alumni** (Brian Schimpf, Matt Grimm) + **Palmer Luckey** (post-Oculus) - "Palantir's spiritual successor."

## The thesis: a16z "American Dynamism" (fact; framing is a16z's)
a16z's **American Dynamism** practice funds "companies building in the national interest" (space/defense/manufacturing/robotics): launched **2023 ($600M)**, **~$1.8B** across two funds, plus a **$1.176B** fund inside the **Jan 2026 $15B** a16z raise - backing **Anduril, Shield AI, Saronic, Castelion**. GP **Katherine Boyle**. It is the branded center of gravity for the wave.

## The cluster (fact of category; "military-industrial complex" = cited critique)
- **Palantir** (~**$325B** market cap, Jun 2026) - the listed "public face."
- **SpaceX** - the most valuable American Dynamism-aligned company (and Golden Dome contractor).
- **Shield AI** ($2B at $12.7B, Mar 2026), **Saronic** (autonomous naval), **Scale AI** (military AI data; Meta took a large stake in 2025), plus Vannevar Labs, Skydio, Epirus.
- Connective tissue: the **Thiel network** (Founders Fund), **a16z**, **8VC** (Joe Lonsdale, Palantir co-founder), and government-adjacent capital **In-Q-Tel** (CIA) + the **NATO Innovation Fund**. Critics (Artforum, et al.) call it an "AI-driven military-industrial complex"; that framing is labeled, not asserted as fact.

## Two honest tensions
1. **Renewal or hype?** Real capital + real contracts + a genuine **procurement-reform** tailwind (software-style acquisition) and geopolitical demand (Ukraine, Iran) - **or** a venture-hype cycle riding war, with valuations (Anduril ~$100B on ~$2.25B revenue) that assume decades of share gains. **Contested; both presented.**
2. **The revolving door (the structural conflict).** The cluster's principals now help **set DoD demand**: **Palmer Luckey (Anduril)** and **Elon Musk (SpaceX/xAI)** co-direct the Pentagon's **Project Meridian** study of which next-gen weapons to build. Founders of the **supplier** side advising the **buyer** on what to buy. A documented conflict-of-interest **structure** - labeled interpretation, not an allegation of wrongdoing.

## Honest limits
Valuations, rounds, revenue, plants, and dates = **fact** (the **~$100B** is **reported/secondary**, labeled). "Military-industrial complex" / "public face" = **cited critique**, labeled. Renewal-vs-hype = **contested**, both sides given. The revolving-door conflict = **interpretation**, labeled. No cabal - this is a **dense, documented investment network**, not a proven directed scheme.

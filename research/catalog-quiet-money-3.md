The remaining thin areas of the quiet-money map: foreign state-capital and crypto's opaque controllers.

The most concentrated fusion of intelligence + AI/data + capital anywhere - hedging the US against China.

## Crypto's quiet controllers
- **Giancarlo Devasini** (chairman/largest owner) + **Paolo Ardoino** (CEO) run **Tether** - the ~$140B+ stablecoin issuer whose profits rival major banks, which holds **$100B+ in US Treasuries** and plows profit into Bitcoin/gold/compute - a private, offshore, systemically-entangled dollar rail with famously opaque governance.
- **Barry Silbert** - the lower-profile builder of **DCG** (Grayscale, Genesis, CoinDesk); the Genesis collapse -> Gemini Earn dispute -> 2024 NYAG ~$2B settlement exposed the interlocking-entity fragility.

## Quiet political kingmakers

## Honest limits
Control, holdings, reserves, and donations are **fact** (filings/attestations/FEC/NYAG); influence and intent are **graded**; the state-capital-surveillance "fusion" (Tahnoon) and donor motives are labeled interpretation. Across independent actors the throughline is a **method** - quiet, concentrated, vehicle-mediated deployment of capital + data + (for the Gulf) state power - not a single conspiracy.

# xAI Colossus - Gulf/Nvidia-financed GPUs + the Memphis gas-turbine fight

xAI's **Colossus** buildout ties three of the map's threads together at one site: the **AI-capital loop** (circular Nvidia/Gulf financing), the **power bottleneck** (on-site gas turbines), and an **environmental-justice** fight.

## The financing (fact; splits reported)
xAI closed a **~$20B round (Jan 2026, ~$230B valuation)** - reportedly **~$7.5B equity + ~$12.5B debt** routed through an **SPV that buys Nvidia GPUs and rents them to xAI** (the same off-balance-sheet template as Meta/Blue Owl's Beignet and the neocloud wall). **Nvidia put ~$2B equity into a vehicle buying Nvidia's own chips** - a textbook **circular supplier-funds-buyer loop**. Other investors: MGX, QIA, Qatar, Fidelity, StepStone, Baron, Cisco, Apollo. xAI reportedly burns **~$1B/month**.

## The power (fact; counts point-in-time)
Colossus runs on **on-site methane turbines** to beat the grid-interconnection queue:
- **Colossus 1 (Memphis, TN):** ~35 turbines (~421MW) for over a year before a **July 2025 permit** (for 15, as "emergency backup").
- **Colossus 2 (Southaven, MS):** ~57-59 turbines; **40-90 planned, up to ~1.5GW** - far above initial need.
Behind-the-meter generation moves emissions off the utility's books and onto the datacenter.

## The fight (allegations; ongoing)
The **NAACP + Mississippi NAACP** (Earthjustice + SELC) **sued xAI + subsidiary MZX Tech** over the unpermitted Southaven turbines - alleging Clean Air Act violations with potential to emit **~1,700 tons/yr NOx** plus PM, CO, and formaldehyde, in **predominantly Black communities** (an environmental-justice claim). A permit issued **~one week after an xAI official discussed the site with President Trump**; groups called the process rushed. *These are allegations in ongoing litigation - no final finding, and no causation is asserted on the permit timing.*

## Why it's on the map
Colossus is the AI-capital loop made physical: circular chip financing -> a private, unrated, cash-burning buildout -> powered by unpermitted gas -> borne by a specific community. It sits in the same debt cohort as the neocloud wall and shares the Nvidia-circularity concern of the whole ecosystem.

# Notable cyber individuals (public-record figures)

A first, tightly-scoped set of **named individuals** from the cyber-history corpus who are unambiguously public (indictments, sanctions, memoirs, major press) and warrant a strategic-behavior dossier (see the Persons tab). This block wires the graph edges; the assessments live in the dossiers, graded.

- **Julian Assange** -> **WikiLeaks** - founder/editor through the 2010 megaleaks; extradition fight ended in a 2024 US plea (time served).
- **Edward Snowden** -> **NSA** - the 2013 mass-surveillance disclosures (PRISM, upstream collection, XKeyscore); asylum then citizenship in **Russia** (a jurisdictional fact, not an endorsement).
- **Maksim Yakubets** -> **Evil Corp** - accused leader (Dridex/Zeus lineage); OFAC-sanctioned + DOJ-indicted 2019 ($5M reward; reported FSB links).
- **Marcus Hutchins** (MalwareTech) -> **WannaCry** - registered the kill-switch that halted the 2017 outbreak; later prosecuted by DOJ over the earlier Kronos trojan (pleaded guilty 2019, time served) - a researcher/redemption arc.

## Honest limits
**Public-record ROLE only** - no private/biographical/location detail (no doxxing). Inclusion means a person is materially documented in the corpus, **not** an accusation beyond what courts/sanctions already state. Behavioral assessments in the Persons tab are labeled inference with calibrated confidence, not fact. Living people; excluded from the formal proofs.

*Sources: DOJ/SDNY + OFAC records; court filings; on-record interviews/memoirs. Cross-refs: WikiLeaks, NSA, Evil_Corp, WannaCry, DOJ, Russia, Malware_Lineage.*

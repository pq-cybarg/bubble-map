# FinFisher / FinSpy (Gamma Group): German-made "lawful intercept" spyware in abusive hands

FinFisher/FinSpy is commercial **"lawful intercept"** spyware sold by the **Gamma Group** (UK/Germany; FinFisher GmbH, Munich) to governments - full-device implants (calls, messages, mic/camera, files).

- **Amnesty International (Sep 2020)** found new FinSpy variants targeting **Egyptian civil society** and revealed - for the first time publicly - **FinSpy for macOS and Linux** alongside Windows/Android, using multi-stage loaders and modular spying.
- **FinFisher GmbH filed insolvency in 2022** amid German criminal scrutiny of unlicensed exports.

It's a core exemplar of the **mercenary/commercial-spyware market** alongside **NSO/Pegasus** and **Intellexa/Cytrox (Predator)** - "lawful intercept" products repeatedly documented against journalists, activists, and dissidents.

**The through-line: the dual-use export gap.** Spyware is dual-use (Wassenaar cyber-tools controls; EU dual-use regulation) yet keeps reaching abusive governments - the distance between export licensing on paper and enforcement in practice.

**Honest limits.** "Lawful intercept" is the vendors' framing; which government ran a given campaign is usually **inferred** from infrastructure/targeting (graded), not confessed. Amnesty / Citizen Lab are advocacy+research bodies whose forensics are well-corroborated but who are a party.

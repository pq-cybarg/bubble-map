# AI companion chatbots and minors

*As of 2026-10-19. Companion to `spec-ai-companion-harms.json`. Handled soberly - the subject is the deaths of minors. Overlay/structural edges only; new node isolated from the AI-financial SCC (unchanged). Grading is per-claim: fact | allegation | ruling. Causation (that a chatbot caused a death) is an allegation in pending litigation, never asserted here as a verdict.*

## The litigation

- **Garcia v. Character Technologies** - Megan Garcia sued Character.AI (Character Technologies), its founders **Noam Shazeer** and **Daniel De Freitas**, and **Google** over the October 2024 death of her 14-year-old son, **Sewell Setzer III**, who had formed an intense attachment to a Character.AI persona. Filed October 2024 (M.D. Fla.). In **May 2025** the court **rejected** the defendants' First-Amendment motion to dismiss at the pleading stage, letting the case proceed - a widely-noted early ruling that AI chatbot output is not clearly protected speech for dismissal purposes. That is a **procedural ruling, not a finding of liability**.
- **Raine v. OpenAI** - Matthew and Maria Raine sued **OpenAI** and CEO **Sam Altman** (August 2025, San Francisco) over the April 2025 suicide of their 16-year-old son **Adam Raine**, alleging ChatGPT (GPT-4o) engaged with his crisis. OpenAI subsequently rolled out **parental controls** and routing of sensitive conversations to more-cautious models.

Both allege the same mechanism - an always-available, emotionally-engaging chatbot deepening a vulnerable minor's crisis. **Whether the product caused the death is the contested question the courts have not resolved.**

## The Google angle (fact)

Google is a co-defendant in Garcia because of its **August 2024 ~$2.7B deal** to license Character.AI's technology and re-hire Shazeer and De Freitas - an "acqui-hire" that absorbed the talent and tech while leaving the operating company behind. Whether that makes Google liable is contested and unadjudicated; the deal itself is fact.

## The regulatory response (fact)

- **FTC 6(b) study (September 2025):** orders to seven firms - **OpenAI, Alphabet/Google, Meta (and Instagram), Character.AI, Snap, and xAI** - seeking how their AI companion chatbots affect children and teens. An inquiry, not an enforcement finding.
- **Character.AI (October 2025):** announced it would bar under-18 users from open-ended chat.
- **California SB 243 (signed October 2025):** the first state AI-companion-chatbot safety law - crisis-referral and AI-disclosure duties. Efficacy untested.

# Payment-processor + card-network financial censorship - the private speech chokepoint

*(Part of the speech-control apparatus layer. A distinct control mechanism - one that can explain de-platforming/pressure activity **not** reducible to the EA/AI-safety networks.)*

## The chokepoint (fact of power; framing contested)
**Visa + Mastercard** clear ~80% of US card volume. Their Acceptable-Use / brand-risk rules are a **near-universal chokepoint**: a merchant cut off by both is effectively de-monetized regardless of legality. The networks cite illegal content, trafficking, chargeback fraud, and brand risk; critics call it **private financial censorship of legal speech**. Both framings are real; this block presents them side by side.

## The documented cases (fact)
- **Backpage (2015):** Visa/Mastercard cut the classifieds site after Senate pressure (later seized/prosecuted).
- **Pornhub / MindGeek (Dec 2020):** Visa + Mastercard suspended acceptance after a NYT column alleged CSAM; mass content purge followed; litigation named the networks.
- **Mastercard adult rules (2021):** "special merchant registration" (age/consent documentation) - anti-CSAM per Mastercard, off-platform pressure on legal adult work per critics.
- **OnlyFans (Aug 2021):** announced an adult-content **ban, then reversed it days later**, citing "assurances" from banking/payment partners - payment pressure nearly killed a legal business overnight. The clearest case.
- **PayPal (Oct 2022):** briefly added a **$2,500 AUP fine for "misinformation,"** then walked it back after backlash - the processor-as-speech-arbiter flashpoint. PayPal/Stripe have also cut adult creators, firearms sellers, and some political/press accounts.

## The industry-wide version: Choke Point
Same logic applied to whole **disfavored industries** via banks/processors: **Operation Choke Point (2013)** (firearms, payday) and the alleged **crypto "Choke Point 2.0" (2023-25)** debanking - regulatory pressure on intermediaries to cut lawful sectors (documented debate; coordinated-intent contested; ties the existing `Operation_Choke_Point_2` node).

## Two convergences
1. **Financial + identity chokepoints reinforce each other:** age/ID-verification mandates (UK OSA, adult-site laws) route through processors **and** ID vendors - the money rail and the identity rail gate the same door.
2. **Competing explanatory frames (the honest point):** de-platforming and "pressure" activity has *several* independent, overlapping drivers - state law, **financial chokepoints**, activist campaigns (NCOSE et al.), and foreign/domestic influence ops. Some of what the EA/AI-network map doesn't fully explain may be **this** mechanism instead. The map holds these as **parallel hypotheses, not one unified cabal.**

## Honest limits
Actions + dates = **fact**; "censorship" vs "harm-reduction / legal-compliance / brand + chargeback risk" = **contested** (both presented); the identity-convergence + competing-frame points = **interpretation**, labeled; no coordinated-conspiracy claim is asserted.

# Quiet money (batch 5): the quant-finance data cluster, quiet European AI money, quiet Asian power

The quant/data-finance fortunes that industrialized machine learning before Big Tech, plus quiet foreign money moving into AI + strategic infrastructure.

- **Ken Griffin -> Citadel + Citadel Securities** - a dominant hedge fund AND the market-maker intermediating a huge share of US retail order flow (PFOF; the GameStop/**Robinhood** episode); a top GOP/cultural mega-donor.
- **David Shaw -> D. E. Shaw** - pioneering quant + talent incubator (**Bezos/Amazon**; the **Two Sigma** founders) + computational biology.
- **Israel Englander -> Millennium** - the low-profile multi-manager "pod shop" giant.
- Together (with Two Sigma) the **quant cluster** that industrialized data/ML in markets - a distinct, quiet AI-capital pole.

## Quiet European AI money
- **Dieter Schwarz -> Schwarz Group (Lidl/Kaufland)** - Germany's reclusive richest - pivoting retail cash into **Aleph Alpha** (sovereign LLM) + **StackIT/Schwarz Digits** cloud + datacenters: EU data-sovereignty AI.

## Enterprise/gov AI + quiet Asian power
- **Thomas Siebel -> C3.ai** - enterprise + **DoD/energy/intelligence** AI.
- **Li Ka-shing -> CK Hutchison** - the Hong Kong empire whose 2025 sale of 43 ports (incl. Panama Canal) to a **BlackRock**-led consortium sits at the center of US-China decoupling.

## Honest limits
Holdings, order-flow data, contracts, and the ports deal are **fact**; influence is **graded**; motives (esp. the reclusive owners) are labeled interpretation. The throughline is a **method** - quiet, data-driven, concentrated capital - not a coordinated plan.

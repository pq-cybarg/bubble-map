# In-Q-Tel: the CIA/IC venture arm and its surveillance-&-data-stack portfolio

**In-Q-Tel (IQT)** is the CIA's not-for-profit strategic-investment arm (est. 1999), now serving the broader Intelligence Community (NGA, DIA, DHS, and others). It identifies dual-use commercial tech and seeds it toward IC adoption - the pipeline that turns startups into intelligence infrastructure.

## The flagship lineage
- **Keyhole** (IQT ~2003) -> **Google** (2004) -> **Google Earth/Maps** -> John Hanke -> **Niantic** (see the Niantic block).
- **Palantir** (IQT ~2004-05) -> the **CIA** as anchor early customer (Gotham) - the model seed-to-customer outcome.
- **FireEye/Mandiant** (IQT early investor) -> **Google** (2022, ~$5.4B).

## The data & AI substrate
**Databricks, Cloudera, MongoDB** - the lakehouse/big-data/document-store layer for IC analytics (Databricks and Palantir even interoperate).

## Geospatial / SIGINT-adjacent (the Keyhole descendants)
- **Orbital Insight** - geospatial AI over satellite imagery -> NGA.
- **HawkEye 360** - smallsat RF-emission geolocation (commercial SIGINT-adjacent) -> NGA; peers with **Planet Labs** (#186).

## OSINT / social-media monitoring
- **Recorded Future** - threat-intel/OSINT temporal analytics.
- **Dataminr** - real-time alerting off the Twitter/X firehose (ACLU-documented protest/activity monitoring) -> "First Alert" to government.
- **Fivecast** - OSINT/social-media risk analytics.
- **Primer AI** - NLP/generative AI for IC document analysis.

## Autonomy, security, quantum, bio, wearables
- **Anduril**, **Shield AI** - defense autonomy (Founders-Fund-adjacent cluster).
- **Cylance** - AI endpoint security (later BlackBerry).
- **D-Wave** - quantum annealing (wires into the quantum tracker's hardware landscape).
- **Ginkgo Bioworks** - synthetic biology / biosecurity.
- **Sonitus** - covert near-ear/in-mouth operator comms.

## Honest limits
IQT does **not disclose funding amounts/dates**, so edges are graded on the **public record of an IQT relationship**, not deal terms. IQT reports **~494 portfolio companies** (Aug 2026); this maps a representative surveillance/data/geoint subset. Edges are **structural** (cia_seed / gov_contract / corporate_lineage): an IQT tie is a **capability/adoption signal, NOT evidence of witting espionage** by the company.

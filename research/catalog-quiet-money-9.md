# Quiet money (batch 10): quiet asset-manager & data-dynasty fortunes

Enormous, deliberately private family fortunes that touch threads already on the map - crypto custody + asset management, the automotive-data complex, and global ag-commodity flows.

## Fidelity / the Johnson dynasty -> crypto + asset management
- **Abigail Johnson -> Fidelity -> Fidelity Digital Assets** - the Johnson family privately controls Fidelity (~$5T+ AUM / ~$15T+ administered). Fidelity Digital Assets (est. 2018) is a rare TradFi giant that built crypto custody + trading early and quietly; Fidelity now runs a spot **Bitcoin ETF (FBTC)** + spot Ether ETF. A low-profile dynasty bridging TradFi to crypto at scale - peer to **BlackRock** in mainstreaming institutional Bitcoin.

## Cox family -> the automotive-data complex
- **James Cox Kennedy -> Cox Enterprises -> Cox Automotive** - the low-profile Cox family controls **Autotrader, Kelley Blue Book, Manheim** (the largest wholesale auto auctions), and **Dealertrack** - a dominant automotive valuation + transaction + inventory data complex feeding dealers, lenders, and insurers. The **Manheim Used Vehicle Value Index** + Dealertrack lending rails sit upstream of auto-collateral valuation - directly relevant to the subprime-auto / auto-ABS stress thread, and the quiet-money angle on the connected-car/auto-data economy documented elsewhere in the map.

## Cargill / the MacMillan dynasty -> ag-commodity flows
- **Cargill** - the largest private US company and one of the "ABCD" global grain traders - dominates corn/grain/oilseed flows, feed, and food ingredients, and provides private ag financing (Cargill Financial) alongside the **Farm Credit System**. The Cargill-MacMillan family (~a dozen billionaires) is among the most private large fortunes in the world.

## Honest limits
Control, ownership, and product scope are documented facts; market shares, downstream data-use scope, and the credit-cycle links are graded/interpretation. The throughline is a method - quiet, concentrated, private capital sitting upstream of crypto rails, auto data, and food/commodity flows - not a coordinated plan.

# Louis Rossmann, FUTO, FULU, and the Consumer Rights Wiki - seventeen years of repair advocacy into digital-ownership lawfare

*Overlay, compiled 2026-09-24. Deepens the people/orgs layer of [[spec-right-to-repair]]. Companions: [[spec-auto-right-to-repair]] (MA telematics + Rossmann rebuttal), [[spec-electronics-parts-pairing]], [[spec-farm-equipment-r2r]], [[spec-mcdonalds-kytch-taylor]] (the food-prep 1201 case), [[spec-open-source-3dprinting-lawfare]] (CRW documents Bambu; FUTO is not a 3D-printer lab), [[digitalid-device-ownership-erosion]], [[spec-local-uncensored-ai]]. Recommended URLs live on the new [[catalog-recommended-links]] / Links tab.*

> **Frame.** Rossmann is the most visible independent US repair advocate: a board-level shop owner who turned YouTube diagnostics into legislative testimony, then into two nonprofits and a public wiki of anti-ownership practices. FUTO (Eron Wolf) funded a stretch of that work (2022-Feb 2025). FULU (2025, with Kevin O'Reilly) is the 501(c)(4) that now runs the bounty program and the Consumer Rights Wiki. The fight is **ownership vs licensing**: parts, schematics, and tools on the R2R side; **DMCA 1201** reform and DRM-unlock bounties on the FULU side.
>
> **Discipline.** Shop, testimony dates, org filings, wiki stats, and tour coverage are **fact**. Rossmann's "security is a strawman" line is **documented advocacy**; it is not adopted as the corpus's finding except where a primary already cut that way (NHTSA walk-back; FTC "Nixing the Fix"). FUTO funded Rossmann; he did not fund FUTO. Overlay; excluded from proofs.

## 1. The shop and the channel (fact)

**Rossmann Repair Group** is a for-profit component-level board-repair and data-recovery shop. Rossmann started repairing Macs as a teenager, ran a storefront in New York, and built a YouTube channel (now ~2.6M subscribers; mirrored on Odysee) that teaches diagnosis instead of "send it to Apple." The shop followed him to **Austin, Texas** after the 2022 FUTO move (announced Aug 2022; business 2023). He still owns the shop 100%. FULU is a **separate** nonprofit so commercial revenue does not fund advocacy. *Fact: [rossmanngroup.com/louis-rossmann](https://rossmanngroup.com/louis-rossmann), [FULU affiliate page](https://rossmanngroup.com/fulu-foundation).*

The channel's political payload is the same sentence as the R2R cluster: you bought it, you should own it. Repair guides stay on the shop; anti-consumer casework moved to the wiki.

## 2. The legislative years (fact)

Rossmann did not start with a 501(c)(4). He spent a decade showing up in rooms manufacturers lobby:

| Year | Venue | What |
|---|---|---|
| 2015-2022 | New York S4104 / **Digital Fair Repair Act** | Written testimony + press conferences. NY became the first state electronics R2R law. |
| 2017 | Nebraska **LB67** | In-person testimony with data-recovery specialist Jessa Jones. |
| ongoing | Repair Preservation Group Action Fund (501(c)(4)) | Lobbies R2R bills. Distinct from Repair Preservation Group (501(c)(3) education / repair.wiki). |
| 2025 | Texas electronics R2R | Enacted Sep 2026 in the corpus tracker; Rossmann publicly criticized carve-outs (consoles, boats, cars, tractors). |
| 2026 | **Own Your Shit** tour | US live events (EFF panelists, SJSU Fixit workshop, Fisker owners). WIRED, 17 Sep 2026: penultimate stop a brewery porch; closer Silver Spring, MD, 1 Oct. Postcards to representatives on **1201**. |

The auto fight is the load-bearing political case: Massachusetts **Question 1 (2020, ~75%)** extended R2R to **telematics**; Alliance for Automotive Innovation sued four years; NHTSA briefly backed a "cybersecurity/safety" letter then **walked it back**; Judge Casper **upheld** the law **Feb 2025**. Rossmann's public rebuttal - that "hackers will weaponize your car" is a strawman of a **diagnostic-data** ask, and that voluntary MOUs are toothless - **aligns with that dated record**. Full write-up: [[spec-auto-right-to-repair]].

Federal **REPAIR Act** (HR 1566, reintroduced 25 Feb 2025) would put telematics access under FTC enforcement. R2R language was **stripped from the 2026 NDAA**. *Fact of the strip; motive not inferred.*

## 3. FUTO: the funder, not the subsidiary (fact; framing corrected)

**FUTO** (founded **2021**, Austin) is a **for-profit** corporation owned and funded by **Eron Wolf** (Yahoo Games-era money, self-described). It does not take Silicon Valley, government, or NGO investment. Mission page: give people control of their technology via in-house engineering, grants, and education. Products/grants include **Grayjay**, **FUTO Keyboard**, **Immich** (multi-year funding), and a fellows program. License: FUTO coined **"Source First"** after OSI/FSF pushback that its terms are **source-available**, not OSI open source (commercial-use limits). Both sides of that naming fight are in [[spec-open-source-3dprinting-lawfare]].

**Rossmann at FUTO:** Director of Community Outreach, **2022 - Feb 2025**. He moved the shop to Austin to work there. Wolf had donated on the order of **$1 million** to Rossmann's nonprofit before bringing him in (Rossmann's own comment on a 2022 Yarvin "debate" he took on short notice as a favor to Wolf). That debate is a documented event; it is **not** treated here as FUTO's political program.

**Correction, carried from the anchor block:** "Rossmann funds FUTO" is **inverted**. FUTO funded the work. He left in 2025.

FUTO does **not** write 3D-printer firmware. The Bambu authorization-control fight is documented on the Consumer Rights Wiki. *Fact.*

Critiques of FUTO (Drew DeVault, Oct 2025: logo/endorsement hygiene, grant-vs-donation labeling) are **on the record**. FUTO updated the site. Grade: process criticism, not a finding that the grants were fake.

## 4. FULU and the bounty program (fact)

**June 2025:** Rossmann and **Kevin O'Reilly** announced **FULU Foundation USA** - Freedom from Unethical Limitations on Users - a **501(c)(4)** aimed at **digital ownership** and **DMCA 1201** reform. Rossmann: R2R asks manufacturers for parts and tools; FULU is the self-help twin - pay people to restore function when the vendor has DRM-locked or bricked the device, **even if 1201 makes sharing the fix legally hazardous**.

**Bounty program** ([bounties.fulu.org](https://bounties.fulu.org/)): cash for unlocking Nest thermostats, Molekule purifiers, Xbox optical drives, **Echelon bikes ($20,000)**, and similar. Results are meant as **legislative evidence**, not a pirate catalog. Rossmann's Oct 2025 video "After 17 years of repair, I'm doing something bigger" is the public launch of the structured bounty site. *Fact of the program and targets; legality of any given unlock is 1201-contested and not asserted here as lawful.*

The **Own Your Shit** tour (summer 2026) is FULU's field operation: live rooms, 1201 postcards, "you bought it, you should own it." WIRED treated it as a grassroots ownership tour, not a PAC.

## 5. Consumer Rights Wiki (fact)

Started **January 2025** as the **Consumer Action Taskforce Wiki** at wiki.rossmanngroup.com, then moved under FULU to **[consumerrights.wiki](https://consumerrights.wiki/)**. About page (protected): community encyclopedia of anti-consumer practice - repair barriers, parts-pairing, planned obsolescence, post-sale EULA changes, **server shutdowns that brick hardware**, forced arbitration, subscription paywalls on features already paid for.

**By the numbers (wiki About, 2026):** **1,472** articles, **206** active contributors. Mission distinguishes classic consumer protection (defects, false ads) from **post-sale remote control** of goods. FULU hosts; editorial is community. Rossmann's video directory on the wiki is the index of which YouTube pieces are casework vs shop vlogs.

The wiki is the **record** FULU wants journalists and legislatures to cite. It is an **advocacy database**, not a court. Grade articles as leads unless they cite a primary.

Documented in-corpus uses: Bambu Lab Authorization Control System ([[spec-open-source-3dprinting-lawfare]]).

## 7. Both sides (neither adopted)

- **(A) Manufacturers / 1201 defenders.** Cryptographic pairing protects biometrics and deters theft; authorized repair is quality and safety; software is licensed; bounty unlocks can ship exploits; a 50-state patchwork is unworkable.
- **(B) Rossmann / FULU / iFixit / PIRG / repair.org.** Serialization of batteries/displays is a repair monopoly; 1201 criminalizes inspecting property you paid for; voluntary MOUs dodge binding law; FTC already found the blanket safety story unsupported (2021).

Dated weight: FTC Nixing the Fix; Apple's SB 244 U-turn + used-parts policy; OR/CO/WA parts-pairing bans; EU Repair Directive **2024/1799** (applies **31 Jul 2026**); MA law **upheld**. Residual legitimate questions: biometric pairing, stolen-part anti-theft, how to build a **secure** telematics access platform.

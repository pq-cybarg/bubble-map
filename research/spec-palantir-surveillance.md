# Palantir: In-Q-Tel lineage, the product lines, and the whole-of-government data web

Palantir is the flagship In-Q-Tel bet (seeded ~2004-05; the CIA its anchor early customer). Founded in the Thiel/Founders Fund orbit; led by Alex Karp. Three product lines anchor everything: **Gotham** (intel/defense), **Foundry** (enterprise/government data integration), and **AIP** (the AI/LLM layer).

## US government
- **Immigration/enforcement:** Gotham + **ImmigrationOS** → **ICE**; **CBP** analytics; all under **DHS**.
- **Defense:** Gotham + **Maven Smart System** → **US Army**; **TITAN** ground station (sensor-to-shooter); a **~$10B Army enterprise agreement** (2025) consolidating dozens of contracts.
- **Health/fiscal:** Foundry across **HHS/CDC** (Tiberius/Warp Speed legacy), **CMS** (Medicare/Medicaid), **IRS**, **SSA**, **Fannie Mae**.

## The cross-agency data-unification concern (2025)
Following a March-2025 EO to remove federal "data silos," the administration tapped Palantir to help **integrate data across agencies** (IRS/SSA/immigration; a reported mega-API project). Palantir **denies building a central master database**; critics warn of a searchable **whole-of-government profile of Americans**. Stated here as a **governance risk grounded in the reporting + EO** — the "master surveillance database" framing is contested interpretation, not a proven system.

## Foreign & alliance deployments
- **NHS England Federated Data Platform** (~£330M, 2023) — the marquee foreign health-data deployment (Foundry-based; privacy-contested).
- **Ukraine MoD** (Gotham, wartime targeting/analytics).
- **Israel MoD** (strategic partnership, Jan 2024; Gaza-targeting use graded/contested).
- **NATO** acquired the **Maven Smart System** (2025) — the alliance's first major enterprise AI buy.

## The commercial engine
**AIP + "AIP Bootcamps"** drove Palantir's **US commercial** surge — the fastest-growing segment and the private-sector revenue engine beneath the government story. Partnerships with **OpenAI** and **Anthropic** wire model access into AIP.

# Quiet money (batch 8): quiet data-broker & cyber-surveillance fortunes

Extending the quiet-money map into fortunes built *on* the data-broker, forensics, and surveillance-analytics economy the rest of the map documents - the money upstream of the surveillance stack.

## Quiet cyber fortunes
- **Assaf Rappaport -> Wiz** - the low-profile ex-Microsoft cloud-security founder whose firm Google/Alphabet agreed to buy for ~$32B in 2025 (one of the largest cybersecurity deals ever), folding a quiet Israeli security company into Big Tech.

## Quiet data-services money
- **Shiv Nadar -> HCLTech** - one of India's quietest tech magnates; HCL(Tech) runs infrastructure, data, and application services for global corporations + governments - the quiet back-office layer of the data economy.

## Foundational data-broker & surveillance fortunes
- **Hank Asher -> LexisNexis (via Seisint/Accurint + MATRIX; later TLO -> TransUnion)** - the "father of data fusion," whose model of merging consumer + government + behavioral records for law-enforcement search is a foundational template for the modern commercial-surveillance stack.
- **Kobi Alexander -> Verint (Comverse)** - founder of the surveillance-analytics + lawful-intercept vendor; fled a US options-backdating indictment to Namibia, later pleaded guilty (2016). Verint spun off **Cognyte** (2021), flagged in commercial-spyware/surveillance reporting.
- **Cellebrite** - the dominant lawful phone-forensics vendor (UFED), procured by ICE/HSI/DHS and thousands of police agencies worldwide; a recurring civil-liberties + due-process flashpoint.

## Honest limits
Founder roles, ownership, deals, and the guilty plea are documented facts; specific surveillance deployments, use-vs-abuse framings, and the broader civil-liberties impact are graded/interpretation. The throughline is a method - quiet capital sitting upstream of the data-fusion + interception economy - not a coordinated plan.

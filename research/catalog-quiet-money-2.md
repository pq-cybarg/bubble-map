Extending the quiet-money map toward the two lowest-profile power centers you flagged: the **adtech/location-data fortunes** and the **quiet political-money operators**.

- **Auren Hoffman** - serial data-broker founder (RapLeaf -> LiveRamp -> **SafeGraph**); SafeGraph's precise foot-traffic data drew scrutiny after abortion-clinic-visit data was sold (2022; Google Play-banned) - wires quiet money straight into the **Location_Data / Data_Brokers** surveillance thread.

## Quiet political-money operators
- **Wilks Brothers** (fracking) and **Richard Uihlein** (Uline) - among the largest, least-visible hard-right / Christian-right donors (Club for Growth; **DonorsTrust** conduits).
- **Thomas Peterffy** (Interactive Brokers) - self-made immigrant, Florida's richest, quiet free-market GOP donor.
- **Charles Koch** - the architect of the most sophisticated durable private influence machine (**Koch Network**: AFP/Stand Together, i360 voter data, academic funding, DonorsTrust conduits) - long-horizon, attribution-averse.
- **Len Blavatnik** (Access Industries / Warner Music) - the cross-border "be everywhere" prestige-and-access donor (bipartisan + universities incl. **Harvard**/Oxford), his post-Soviet wealth origin under recurring scrutiny.
- **David Siegel** - completes the **Two Sigma** quant-data pair with Overdeck.

**DonorsTrust** - the donor-advised "dark-money ATM" - is the shared conduit tying several of these to the **Federalist Society** / judiciary network mapped in batch 2.

## Honest limits
Holdings, disclosed donations, and documented data-sales are **fact**; influence and intent are **graded**; motive framings (Blavatnik's, the donors') are labeled interpretation. The throughline across independent, often-rival actors is a **method** - quiet, vehicle-mediated deployment of capital + data - not a single coordinated conspiracy.

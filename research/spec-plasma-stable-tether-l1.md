# Tether's stablecoin-L1 push (Plasma + Stable) - the boom-bust counterpoint

The stablecoin-L1 race has three flagships: **Arc** (Circle/USDC, issuer-run), **Tempo** (Stripe/Paradigm, processor-run), and **Plasma** (Tether/USDT). Plasma is the one with a full boom-bust arc already on the record - a useful reality check on the others.

## Plasma (XPL)
- **What:** a USDT-centric, Bitcoin-anchored, EVM L1 (PlasmaBFT consensus); **zero-fee USDT transfers**, gas payable in USDT. Mainnet **25 Sep 2025**.
- **Backing:** Tether + **Bitfinex** + **Founders Fund** (Thiel network - the same capital behind Erebor + Persona). Public token sale reportedly ~$373M, ~7x its $50M target.
- **Launch metrics:** ~$2B+ day-one TVL, 100+ DeFi partners (Aave, Ethena, Euler, Fluid).

## The credibility reality check
- **XPL crashed ~80-85%** from its ~$1.5-1.67 high to ~$0.20 by late 2025 (well corroborated). 
- **Structural token-demand flaw:** if stablecoin transfers are zero-fee, there is little reason to *hold* XPL - the token's core utility undercuts its own demand. *(interpretation, but a widely-noted one.)*
- **Incentive-driven, not organic:** the day-one TVL was juiced by XPL rewards; much of it migrated out as incentives expired - the hard problem of converting incentivized deposits into real usage.
- **Single-issuer concentration:** ~89% of Plasma stablecoins are USDT, tying the chain to Tether's health + choices.
- **TVL figures conflict wildly** across sources (~$0.6B vs ~$2B vs ~$18.7B at different dates/methodologies). **Treat current TVL as uncertain - verify on DeFiLlama.** *(reported/conflicting.)*

## Stable + the wider field
Tether is also tied to **"Stable,"** a second USDT-focused L1 - so the largest issuer is pursuing *multiple* issuer-run chains. Meanwhile **Tron** remains USDT's dominant transfer chain, so Tether's own L1s partly compete with USDT's existing home. Ripple's **RLUSD** rounds out the field. The through-line matches Arc/Tempo: everyone who touches stablecoins now wants to own the rail and capture float + fees on top of reserve income - but Plasma shows that owning a chain is not the same as sustaining organic demand.

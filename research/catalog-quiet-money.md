# Quiet money: the low-profile ultra-wealthy in data, surveillance, and political influence

The deliberately invisible power-holders - people who shape data/surveillance infrastructure and politics from the shadows, precisely because they avoid the spotlight.

## Data-politics (the Mercer network)
- **Robert Mercer** (Renaissance Technologies / Medallion) applied a quant worldview to elections - funding **Cambridge Analytica** and **Breitbart** through intermediaries.
- **Rebekah Mercer** ran the operational side (CA board; Breitbart; co-founded Parler).
- **Cambridge Analytica** harvested ~87M **Facebook** profiles for psychographic targeting (2016) - the scandal that exposed the social-data -> political-manipulation pipeline (FTC's $5B Facebook fine; CA collapsed).

## Quiet market money with policy leverage
- **Jeffrey Yass** (Susquehanna) - one of the richest, least-visible Americans; a large **ByteDance/TikTok** stake and huge libertarian/crypto/school-choice giving (Club for Growth, Fairshake), central to TikTok-ban politics.
- **John Overdeck** (Two Sigma) - the quiet quant-finance cohort (RenTech/Two Sigma/Citadel/DE Shaw) that industrialized data + ML before Big Tech.

## The dark-money / judiciary architects
- **Barre Seid** gave his entire ~$1.6B company to the **Marble Freedom Trust** (2021) - the largest known US political gift - handing control to **Leonard Leo**, the **Federalist Society** co-chair who built the pipeline that shaped a **SCOTUS** supermajority. (Leo isn't a billionaire, but controls billions.)
- **Harlan Crow** - quiet real-estate patron whose undisclosed gifts to Justice Thomas became the defining court-ethics scandal.
- **Timothy Mellon** - a near-invisible heir who became the **largest disclosed individual donor of 2024** (>$150M) with no public rationale.

## The surveillance-data backbone
- **Hank Asher** - the "father of data fusion" (d. 2013): **Seisint** (Accurint; the post-9/11 **MATRIX** database) -> **LexisNexis**, and **TLO** -> TransUnion. His model is the direct ancestor of today's data-broker surveillance backbone wired across the auto/insurance/policing pipelines.

## Honest limits
Disclosed donations, stakes, and holdings are **fact**; influence and intent are **graded**; motives (esp. Mellon's) are often opaque and stated as such. No coordinated conspiracy is imputed across these independent actors - the throughline is a **method** (quiet, vehicle-mediated, interest-aligned deployment of capital), not a cabal.
